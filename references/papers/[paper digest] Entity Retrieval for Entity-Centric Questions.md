# Entity Retrieval for Answering Entity-Centric Questions - salient-entity lookup replaces document similarity search

**arXiv 2408.02795 (Shavarani & Sarkar, Simon Fraser University)**. Replaces question-document similarity search with a straight lookup: extract the salient entity/entities from the question, fetch their Wikipedia articles directly, truncate to the first W words, and hand that to the LLM - no passage index, no embedding search. With gold question-entity annotations this beats BM25 by **25% on nDCG** (EntityQuestions dev) while running **25% faster than BM25** and needing roughly **10x less main memory and disk than dense retrieval** (ANCE: 64.2GB RAM / 61.5GB disk vs Entity Retrieval: 6.3GB RAM / 9.4GB disk). The catch: without gold annotations, performance is capped by the entity linker's coverage - the SPEL linker (used as the best available off-the-shelf linker) resolves only **56.5% of FactoidQA questions** to an entity (65.6% on EntityQuestions, 74.9% on StrategyQA), so the rest fall back to unaugmented prompting.

**Key mechanism**: for a question, identify its salient entity mentions, look up the corresponding knowledge-base (Wikipedia) article per entity, truncate each to the first W words (50/100/300/1000 tested), and concatenate as the augmentation context - replacing BM25/DPR/ANCE's index-search-and-rank pipeline with direct dictionary lookup. Gold-annotation runs (question entity spans given) establish a performance ceiling; automatic-linking runs substitute SPEL (Shavarani & Sarkar 2023, the top performer per Ong et al. 2024's linker benchmark) since no candidate-set is available at inference.

**Main findings**: with gold annotations, ER1000w's nDCG surpasses BM25 (the strongest baseline) by 25% on EntityQuestions and matches 100-document BM25 on FactoidQA while returning ~1 document per question; ER50w/ER100w post the best EM/F1 on FactoidQA and EntityQuestions dev/test over Closed-book, BM25, DPR, and ANCE (LLaMA-3 8B reader). Real-time cost: BM25 45min/11GB disk/2.3GB RAM, ANCE 960min/61.5GB disk/64.2GB RAM, Entity-Retrieval(SPEL,100w) 34min/9.4GB disk/6.3GB RAM to answer all 2,203 FactoidQA questions. With SPEL substituting for gold annotations, Entity Retrieval still beats BM25/ANCE on EntityQuestions and dominates on StrategyQA (highest accuracy, fewest invalid Yes/No outputs), but the linker's 56.5-74.9% coverage caps how much of the ceiling is reachable, and longer documents (1000w) that helped under gold annotations hurt under automatic linking (noise from imprecise links overwhelms the reader).

**Key takeaways for KGF**: direct evidence that a deterministic entity-lookup (no similarity search, no per-query LLM call) beats sparse and dense retrieval on entity-centric questions at a fraction of the memory/disk footprint - supports KGF's retrieval-first, minimal-hop design when the query names a known entity. The load-bearing constraint is entity-linker coverage, not the lookup mechanism itself: this paper's 56.5% FactoidQA linking rate (SPEL) is the binding cap on how often the method's ceiling performance is even reachable. R47-H509 targets exactly this gap - GLiNER's reported 95.2% linking accuracy would push KGF's graph-entity lookup toward the gold-annotation ceiling this paper measures rather than the SPEL-degraded numbers, and the document-length finding (longer context helps under reliable links, hurts under noisy ones) argues for scaling KGF's lookup context budget with link confidence.
