**On the Relation between Graph Ricci Curvature and Community Structure (2024)**

The heuristic that "negative curvature marks community boundaries" long lacked theory. This paper derives a priori upper bounds on the Ollivier and Forman curvature of inter-community edges, proving they carry more negative curvature than intra-community edges. That gives curvature-thresholded community detection a principled cutoff instead of a tuned heuristic.

**Key mechanism**
- Derives a priori upper bounds on Ollivier/Forman curvature of inter-community edges
- Shows inter-community edges provably carry more negative curvature than intra-community edges
- Turns curvature thresholding into a detector with principled cutoffs

**Main findings**
- Theoretical justification for curvature-thresholded community detection
- Principled cutoffs replace tuned heuristics

**Key takeaways**
- Bridging edges between communities have a bounded, anomalous curvature signature
- The detector is embedding-free
- Pairs naturally with AFRC as the computation engine

**Relevance**
- Most on-target for the cross-type-duplicate defect - cross-type duplicates by construction bridge two type-communities frozen apart by the ontology, so the theorem predicts the bridging edge carries an anomalous bounded curvature signature
