# Concept-SAE: A Controllable and Invertible Concept Interface for Sparse Autoencoders

**Ding, Chen, Zhao, Xu (CUHK), 2025** - arXiv 2509.22015. Framework that supervises a chosen subset of Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) latents onto externally-defined concepts while leaving the remaining latents free for open-ended discovery, reaching **Localization Ratio 1.395-1.472 vs 0.982-1.019 for a concept-embedding baseline (CEM)** across ResNet-18 layers, and **adversarial-sample detection AUC 0.869-0.974** across ViT/ResNet-18 x CelebA/ImageNet-1k, beating six established detection baselines (best baseline AUC 0.828-0.901).

**Key mechanism**: the activation subspace is split into two orthogonal components. Concept Tokens are produced by a Concept Tokenizer that projects vision-model features into a per-concept space and predicts, for each of n predefined concepts, a binary existence score (sigmoid) and a spatial segmentation mask; supervision comes from a VLM (existence labels) and a segmentation model (masks), fused so VLM-absent concepts zero out their mask. A frozen-tokenizer Concept Aggregator then decodes concept score + mask back to the original feature via an MLP, with a KL-divergence term aligning the tokenizer's merge weights and the aggregator's weights, plus L1 sparsity on both. A separate Free Tokenizer/Aggregator pair, trained like a standard unsupervised SAE, absorbs everything outside the predefined concept pool and sums with the concept reconstruction. Training runs in three stages: tokenizer, then aggregator, then the free modules jointly.

**Main findings**:
- RQ1 (faithfulness/localization): Concept-SAE's Localization Ratio beats CEM at every tested ResNet-18 layer - layer 5: 1.472 vs 1.019; layer 7: 1.402 vs 0.982; layer 9: 1.395 vs 1.002 (Table 1)
- RQ2 (detection): using entropy of concept scores as an adversarial-sample (FGSM) detector, AUC is 0.974 (ViT/CelebA), 0.940 (ResNet-18/CelebA), 0.948 (ViT/ImageNet), 0.869 (ResNet-18/ImageNet) - ahead of JPEG, Randomization, Deflection, Denoise, VLAD, and Feature Squeezing baselines on every cell (best baseline tops out at 0.901, 0.896, 0.876, 0.828 respectively) (Table 3)
- RQ3 (controllability): setting concept scores for beard/mustache/adam's-apple to 1.0 or 0.0, decoding through the aggregator, and re-injecting the synthetic feature into the vision model corrects CelebA gender misclassifications; interventions at deeper ViT layers are more effective, while both shallow and deep ResNet-18 layers correct predictions
- RQ4 (stability): Jensen-Shannon distance between clean and adversarial concept-score distributions flags the most vulnerable layers; layer-wise finetuning targeted at high-JS layers yields the largest accuracy recovery, e.g. CelebA ResNet-18 all-layers finetune 39.75% -> 80.52% (+40.77 pp), ImageNet ResNet-18 all-layers 9.51% -> 34.71% (+25.20 pp) (Table 4)
- Evaluated on CelebA (gender binary classification) and ImageNet-1k (1000-way), over ResNet-18 (trained) and ViT-B/32 (LAION-2B pretrained, zero-shot)

**Key takeaways for KGF**: this is the direct mechanism reference for R47-H504's GLiNER-anchored typed atoms - Concept-SAE proves that pinning a subset of SAE/autoencoder latent dimensions to externally-named concepts (dual supervision: existence + localization, not a single presence bit) keeps those dimensions faithful and disentangled while a parallel free-token block still supports unconstrained emergent discovery, exactly the split H504 wants between typed and untyped atom dimensions. The orthogonality is not just a loss term - it is enforced by staged training (freeze tokenizer before training aggregator; jointly train free modules last) plus explicit L1 sparsity and a KL term aligning merge/aggregation weights, which is the concrete recipe to port. Caveat: every result here is vision-domain (CelebA faces, ImageNet, ResNet-18/ViT-B32) with no text or entity-typing experiment - the transfer to GLiNER-anchored named sub-concept dimensions in a text/graph setting is an extrapolation. The authors' own limitation section flags that the approach depends on upstream VLM/segmentation label quality and is architecturally suited to localizable (spatially maskable) concepts; abstract or textual concepts without a spatial analogue are named as unsolved future work, which is precisely the regime KGF's typed-atom use case sits in.
