# GLiNER: Generalist Model for Named Entity Recognition using Bidirectional Transformer

**arXiv 2311.08526 (Nov 2023)**. Encoder-only open-type NER: a DeBERTa-v3 bidirectional transformer matches span representations against entity-type embeddings in a shared latent space, replacing autoregressive generation with a single parallel forward pass. GLiNER-L (0.3B params) scores **60.9 avg F1** zero-shot on the 7-dataset OOD NER Benchmark, beating GoLLIE-7B (58.0), UniNER-13B (55.6) and ChatGPT (47.5); GLiNER-S at just **50M params** (52.7 avg) already beats ChatGPT and 11B InstructUIE (47.2).

**Key mechanism**: input is a single sequence - a learned `[ENT]` token before each candidate entity-type name, a `[SEP]` delimiter, then the raw text - encoded jointly by the BiLM so type prompts and text tokens attend to each other bidirectionally. Entity-type embeddings `q_t` come from a two-layer FFN over each `[ENT]` output; span embeddings `S_ij = FFN(h_i ⊗ h_j)` are computed for every span up to length K=12 (bounds complexity to linear, no recall loss reported). The match score `φ(i,j,t) = σ(S_ij^T q_t)` is a sigmoid dot-product trained with binary cross-entropy over all span/type pairs - a native, calibrated-by-training probability per span-type pair, not a post-hoc softmax over a fixed label set. Decoding is a greedy priority-queue span selector, O(n log n), at threshold 0.5, with two modes: flat (highest-scoring non-overlapping spans) and nested (fully-nested spans permitted, partial overlaps still excluded).

**Main findings**:
- Zero-shot OOD NER Benchmark (Table 1, 7 CrossNER/MIT datasets): GLiNER-L 60.9 avg vs GoLLIE-7B 58.0, UniNER-13B 55.6, UniNER-7B 53.7, ChatGPT 47.5, InstructUIE-11B 47.2, USM-0.3B 37.8. GLiNER-M (90M) reaches 55.4, matching UniNER-13B (55.6) at ~140x fewer parameters; GLiNER-L beats UniNER-13B by ~5 points
- Zero-shot 20 NER Benchmark (Table 2, biomedical/news/tweet domains): GLiNER-L 47.8 avg, best score on 13 of 20 datasets, ~2 points above UniNER-7B (45.7) and well above ChatGPT (36.5); GLiNER underperforms UniNER specifically on tweet-based datasets
- Zero-shot multilingual (Multiconer, Table 3, 11 languages): GLiNER surpasses ChatGPT in 8 of 10 languages absent from its training data; GLiNER-Multi is stronger on non-Latin scripts than the English-backbone GLiNER-En variant
- In-domain supervised fine-tuning (Table 4, 20 datasets): Pile-NER-pretrained GLiNER-L averages 0.8 points above the non-pretrained variant, and 0.9 points above InstructUIE (11B, ~30x larger) despite matching its fine-tuning data; falls behind UniNER-7B by almost 3 points but still takes best score on 7 of 20 datasets
- Ablations: 50% negative-entity-type sampling during training is the sweet spot (F1 60.9, Prec 62.3/Rec 59.7) vs 0% negatives (F1 53.3, high recall/low precision - model over-predicts) vs 75% negatives (F1 58.6, more cautious, lower recall); random entity-type dropping during training adds +1.4 F1 OOD average; DeBERTa-v3 is the strongest backbone tested, XLNet failed outright (<=3 F1 despite tuning)
- Training data: Pile-NER, 44,889 passages / 240k entity spans / 13k distinct entity types, ChatGPT-extracted (no type list given to the LLM) from 50,000 Pile-sampled texts; GLiNER-L trains in 5 hours on one A100

**Key takeaways for KGF**: this is the exact architecture behind KGF's H260 asset (95.2% carrier recall) and the substrate for the entire R47 sparse-graph-coding axis. The span-representation-vs-type-embedding matching score gives deterministic, single-pass extraction with no autoregressive per-token generation cost, and the sigmoid match score is a native per-entity confidence signal usable directly for downstream gating without a separate calibration model. The nested-NER decode mode is a direct fit where KGF spans overlap (a sub-entity nested inside a larger typed span). Most load-bearing for KGF's open-type regime: GLiNER never sees KGF's target types during training yet generalizes competitively against 7-13B LLM baselines at 0.3B parameters - the same zero-shot, unseen-type extraction posture KGF operates under, evidenced here without any target-domain fine-tuning.
