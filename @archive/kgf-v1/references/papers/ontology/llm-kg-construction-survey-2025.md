# LLM-Empowered Knowledge Graph Construction and Reasoning: A Survey

- **Authors**: Bian et al.
- **Venue**: arXiv preprint, 2025
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20345
- **Key contribution**: Comprehensive survey covering LLM-based knowledge graph construction including ontology alignment, type hierarchy discovery, and entity resolution. Catalogs approaches for using LLMs to discover and refine ontological structures from text, including iterative schema evolution and type hierarchy induction.
- **Relevance to H5g**: Survey evidence that LLM-driven ontology evolution (discovering type hierarchies from data rather than pre-specifying them) is an active research direction. Validates the fluid-mode approach where hierarchy starts empty and is discovered from cross-type duplicate patterns across documents.
