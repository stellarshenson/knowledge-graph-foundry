# Knowledge Graph Foundry - SOTA Design

**Canonical SOTA Document**

The distilled design of the shipped engine: components that survived hypothesis adjudication, with the measurement system that governs every change. Evidence lives in the canonical experiments log ([kgf-redesign-experiments.md](experiments/kgf-redesign-experiments.md)) and the promotions ledger ([sota-promotions.md](sota-promotions.md)); this document carries only what ships. Design sections are added as campaigns converge - the Metrics section leads because the measurement system is the part every other section is judged by.

## Metrics

Every KGF change is adjudicated by instruments, not impressions - each metric below states what it measures, how, and why it earns its place. A delta counts only when it clears the instrument's measured noise floor (the H351 rule: bands priced off run-to-run variance, never magic tolerances).

### Retrieval and answer quality

- **Evidence recall (probe harness)** - what: share of gold evidence present in the retrieved context, per probe, averaged over the probe set - how: gold strings matched in the normalized joined context (`notebooks/h158_measure.py`), engine-parity retrieval (overfetch stamped in every report, DEF-14) - why: retrieval quality independent of the reader; the primary lever-adjudication metric of every R-round
- **Entailment sufficiency (H172)** - what: whether retrieved context semantically entails each atomic gold unit - how: mDeBERTa NLI cross-encoder, atomic-unit max-entailment, 1.8 ms/pair, 94% human agreement - why: string matching misses paraphrase; this is the benchmark instrument for sufficiency claims, and any conclusion generated with the retired fuzzy matcher is flagged for re-adjudication
- **Probe-set discipline (H186)** - what: a constraint, not a number - how: probe golds must be document-grounded, never graph-derived - why: graph-derived golds saturate at 99.2% and erase the very levers under test
- **Benchmark EM / F1 / recall@5 (#59, incoming)** - what: answer exact-match and token-F1 (HotpotQA normalization), gold-passage recall in the top 5 - how: `scripts/bench_score.py` over the QA adapter's answers JSONL, sealed 1000-question sets, one shot - why: the public comparison surface against GraphRAG / LightRAG / HippoRAG-2 published numbers
- **Tokens per answered query (#59, incoming)** - what: retrieval context tokens + LLM call count per question - how: logged by the QA adapter per question from the first version - why: the peer table prices nothing; single-shot retrieval at a fraction of an agentic walk's bill (PoG: 8,156 tok / 13.3 calls per question) is a headline claim only a cost column can express

### Graph quality and identity

- **Identity precision (H101 benchmark)** - what: SAME_AS decision quality - how: 298 adjudicated pairs with evidence strings; precision proxy on live edges - why: false merges corrupt silently; the adjudicated set is the ground truth the resolver is scored against
- **Calibration ECE** - what: whether the resolver's posterior means what it says - how: isotonic-calibrated Titan cosine on adjudicated labels (ECE 0.050); per-corpus refit mandated by the H157 transfer failure (5.18x ECE cross-corpus) - why: a mis-calibrated confidence poisons every downstream threshold
- **Extraction variance (H107)** - what: run-to-run instability of what the extractor emits - how: repeated single-chunk extraction, Jaccard over emissions - why: measured at 71%, the dominant identity root cause; the metric that adjudicates every determinism lever
- **Coverage certificate (R39-H389, instrument live)** - what: per-document fact coverage - the fraction of source facts the graph actually holds - plus a named miss list - how: verbatim-grounded fact probes per chunk, graph-support test, KGGen-MINE style adjudication; first live certificates 64.1% / 62.1% on the 2wiki pile at ~10% of ingest wall; the +-2% reproducibility bar understates observed same-graph spread at 25-doc scale (R45 caveat), registered adjudication pending - why: every published extractor silently drops 34-70% of source facts; "does not miss information" is a claim only a certificate can back

### Operations

- **Throughput (gen tok/s + chunks/min)** - what: real document-grinding rate - how: vLLM counter deltas over stability-gated 600s windows with an occupancy guard >= 0.95c (H388 fast rung, PARTIAL - parity re-registers on gen tok/s at N>=3 windows); total tok/s rejected as the metric, chunks/min carries ~20%+ replication spread - why: feeds the H362 calibrator; ingest runs at a measured operating point (c=56 on the plateau [48,60], 3.4x the old default), not a guess
- **Latency envelope (p50/p95/max per chunk)** - what: per-request wall time at the operating point - how: recorded per calibration rung - why: a knee whose latency violates the client timeout is not shippable (H342); sets `llm.timeout`
- **Warm-start band** - what: is the cached calibration still true - how (calibrator shipped in `extraction/throughput.py`; H362 verdict withheld pending the false-trigger n=10 and perturbation clauses): first real window vs cached mean +-3 sigma with sigma MEASURED from repeat windows (0.557 from the N=3 c=56 windows; the magic fallback tolerance was deleted under adversarial review) - why: trust-but-verify; a changed setup recalibrates instead of silently underperforming
- **Progressive regression trajectory** - what: whether growing the graph degrades previously-working queries - how: external prober (`scripts/bench_progressive_probe.py`) round-robins over all eligible benchmark questions (those whose gold passages are fully ingested), a capped sample per cycle, through the public `Foundry.probe()` retrieval-only surface; pass baselines persist across restarts via the trajectory file; a pass -> fail transition is a flagged regression - why: scale-induced regressions (identity collisions, ranking dilution) are invisible to end-state evaluation; the trajectory catches them at the document where they begin

### Gate and abstention

- **Escalation gate signal** - what: per-query "is the base render sufficient" - how: top-seed index score against a CRC-fitted threshold (alpha = 0.08 certificate, fitted at optimize-time, checked at query-time for one float comparison) - why: the only sanctioned second retrieval round; alpha bounds the escalation rate so the second hop stays rare and priced
- **Miss / abstention detection** - what: queries the graph cannot answer - how: top-seed similarity short-circuit (86.7% miss detection, 0% false abstention, 95% token cut on the miss class, H181) - why: honest "not in corpus" beats a confident hallucination and costs almost nothing

### Measurement discipline

- **Noise floors before verdicts** - every continuous metric carries a measured run-to-run sigma; a hypothesis delta below its instrument's band is INCONCLUSIVE, never CONFIRMED (H351; DEF-11 is the standing counterexample)
- **Per-attempt counting** - retries mask storms: throughput and throttle instruments count raw request ATTEMPTS at the client, never goodput (H341's contamination is the standing counterexample)
- **Instrument-first sequencing** - rounds that need a new metric build and validate the instrument before the levers (R39 sequences H389 before every coverage hypothesis)
- **Resolution roadmap** - the 24-probe harness is saturated (23/24); gradient-tracking metrics with power analysis at 1000-question scale are registered (R42, H410-H417: metric registry with measured noise floors, paired clustered-SE A/Bs, judge noise pricing)

## Ingest and extraction

The ingest path is parse -> chunk -> enumerate-then-extract -> resolve, run at a calibrated GPU operating point. Chunking survived adjudication essentially unchanged; the extraction recipe and the feeding rate did not.

- **Chunking exonerated (R32-H354 census gate, H235)** - the entire chunking round closed pre-build: every splitter hypothesis (H355-H361) measured 0% target-class failure mass vs the 10% bar, and chunk-boundary churn is refuted as a variance driver (H235); whole-document extraction (H36) is superseded - contraindicated by the lost-in-the-middle mid-context drop and GraphRAG's large-window yield loss
- **Header carryover (H153)** - the one shipped chunker fix: all 64 severed table rows re-associated with their headers at 0.258% token overhead, zero false header injections
- **Extraction recipe: enumerate-then-extract (H246, composition-confirmed by H240b)** - one "list every entity name, terse" call + one primed extract call reaches 97.1% of union-of-5 coverage at 1.05x single-pass cost; won the live scratch-ingest A/B against mention-emission (recall 0.7292 vs 0.6667, SAME_AS precision 0.5517 vs 0.48) - H258's offline "strict dominance" did not survive composition (1.17x entity inflation fragmented identity)
- **Variance is model-inherent; only task restructuring moves it** - temp-0 extraction runs at ~0.60 mean pairwise Jaccard distance in quiet conditions (H229); seed pinning yields zero identical reproductions (H232 - its 6-call gate is the standing per-engine-upgrade canary); guided-JSON decoding worsens variance 21% (H233); instructing, cueing, or demonstrating exhaustivity all fail (H243/H248/H261 refuted) - the prior moves only by restructuring the task (H246)
- **Embedding cache (DEF-1, fixed)** - (provider, model, text)-keyed cache in `generate_embeddings`; the defect state re-embedded every mention per document (one 9-chunk manual = 708 Bedrock calls)
- **Text-column routing (DEF-2, fixed)** - a structured file with any text-heavy column (median cell length > `ingest.text_column_median_chars`, default 200) expands to one document per row through the chunk-and-extract path; the defect state ingested a 481-article .jsonl as one document with zero LLM reading
- **Concurrency auto-calibration (H362/H363/H388)** - `extraction.auto_calibrate` resolves the cached knee per full inference identity (engine -> endpoint -> model ID -> extraction-config hash); operating point c=56 on the plateau [48, 60] (indistinguishable at current power), hard capacity cliff at c=64, 3.4x the old c=8 default; the H388 fast rung (steady-state counter windows, wall <= 1/3) is the calibrator's probe routine at PARTIAL - its parity claim re-registers on gen tok/s at N>=3 windows; H362's own verdict is withheld pending the false-trigger and perturbation clauses
- **Glyph normalization (H190)** - parser post-process + resolver name-identity detector: 69/69 previously-absent names recovered, exactly 0 false conflations on the 2797-name vocabulary
- **Parser decision (H146-H151, H150, H217)** - a TEXT-layer swap, not vision: pypdf recovers 93.7% of the 95 lost names at ~0.03 s/page as pymupdf4llm's union partner; the H51 name-recall harness is the standing acceptance test for any parser change; pixels claim zero of the benchmark coverage ceiling (H217)
- **Image ingestion, per-class (H216/H218/H224)** - Docling primary + page-render for vector-drawn content; the decorative filter (recurrence + size) routes only 3% of the image stream to the VLM; describe-then-extract is GO for diagrams + rendered tables, product photos BLOCKED pending the H291 brand guard (brand-hallucination precision failure)

## Ontology lifecycle

Curing timing comes from the evidence, not constants; drift detection is doubly validated but its rebuild path is an honestly open defect.

- **Good-Turing UCB curing gate (H32, DEF-3 closed)** - missing-mass upper-confidence-bound gate on both cure paths: scale-free, truncation-invariant, no count constants; 98.55% forward mass coverage at its cure point vs 85.14% for the v1 plateau gate; validated at scale by H33 - cure at doc 7 held 99.96% forward coverage over 474 unseen documents
- **Lifecycle transfers across corpus classes (H157)** - the gate fired at doc 13 / 51 types on the paper corpus vs doc 4 / 12 on the device corpus - later and wider on the denser genre, zero spurious recures, FSM held STABLE
- **Drift detector: the production all-3-consecutive criterion (H50/H59/H120)** - retained after losing two formal challenges to the CUSUM class: it matched CUSUM at delta=0.30 (H50) and correctly ignores the benign one-document full-remap spike an LLR variant false-alarms on (H59) - persistence, not evidence weighting, distinguishes a transient from a shift
- **Rebuild path honestly open (DEF-9 / R36-H378)** - the shipped rebuild conjunction (JSD AND 3-consecutive remap) is structurally unreachable (anti-phase signals; never fired even at JSD 0.5079 = 3.4x threshold); the H317 CUSUM is wired behind `drift.cusum_enabled` (default off) with the RECURING exit implemented (`end_recure` type adoption + detector re-baseline, unit-pinned on the anti-phase, slow-ramp and livelock cases) - DEF-9 stays open pending the end-to-end induced-drift replay
- **Ontology value is upstream only (H174)** - the type system is task-decorative for adjacency/retrieval (type edits move bits, never recall); it earns its keep at extraction/typing time

## Identity and resolution

Shipped identity is the v1 Bayesian resolver plus a demotion court; the calibrated v2 stack is fully wired behind a flag, repeatedly better on merit, and its default flip stays gate-blocked by its own registered bars.

- **Shipped default: v1 resolver + demote-don't-delete court (H290/H282/H288)** - a single-shot judge detects 98.0% of SAME_AS false merges at 513 tok/pair; judged-false edges DEMOTE to soft SIMILAR_TO links instead of deleting, and measured merge-vs-soft-link render equivalence (+0.0 pts, H288) makes over-splits structurally costless; effective post-court false-merge rate 0.0073 (R29 / DEF-10 closed); deliberation past K~3 calls is a measured anti-lever (H284)
- **v2 calibrated stack wired, not default (H106/H129/H158; H241/H340 gates)** - isotonic-calibrated Titan cosine (ECE 0.050) + global NLI contradiction veto (83% false-merge cut at 2.4% scoring cost) + logistic arbitration over detectors (F1 0.811, false merges 39 -> 11), behind `resolution.identity_stack`; the default flip failed its registered gates twice (H241 PARTIAL: 1.57x vs the 2x false-merge bar; H340 NO-GO on a gate form since proven near-uninformative) - GAP-1 keeps the question open on merit (v2 precision 5.9x, false-merge rate 2.0x lower, recall exact tie), reopenable only under a new pre-registered decider with the H351 N>=3 Welch-CI form; the Bayesian posterior is retired from the v2 decision path (AUC 0.117 vs truth) while v1's machinery survived its own demolition case (H54: two rules reproduce only 74.4% of its decisions)
- **Titan cosine ranking unbeaten (R12 bake-off)** - seven challenger classes lost (cross-encoders -0.04, local bi-encoders -0.19, instruction-7B -0.34, NLI -0.26, field split ~0, structure -0.53, ensemble +0.004); the identity fix is calibration, never the scorer
- **Per-corpus calibration is mandatory (H157)** - transferred isotonic constants hit ECE 0.2606 = 5.18x the held-out reference; constants are corpus-class-bound (decisions transfer, probabilities do not)
- **Series-fragment merge + unanimous-spec hoist (H364/H365)** - 83.3% of emitted-but-lost probe mass localized to the resolution stage; the repair (merge series fragments sharing a model-code stem, hoist `prop_*` keys carried identically by EVERY child onto series-marker parents, no-overwrite, exactly reversible) flipped P09 0.0 -> 1.0 and P16 0.5 -> 1.0 with 22 probes bit-identical (mean 0.7917 -> 0.8542); generalized in `graph/hoist.py` behind `resolution.spec_hoist` (default off pending the clean-rebuild verification); the motivating fragment pair sits at cosine 0.7477 - below every resolver threshold, invisible to embedding kNN
- **Soft similarity links (H268; read path corrected by H370)** - defer-zone pairs materialize as posterior-weighted SIMILAR_TO edges (link, never merge), closing 64.7% of sibling-fragment attachment misses at zero false-merge risk; H370 later refuted 1-hop SIMILAR_TO render expansion on this graph (flat forward) - the links' shipped value is as demotion target and attachment substrate, not traversal
- **Extraction variance is the standing identity constraint (H107 -> H119/H229/H231/H267)** - the original "71% surface-form variance" described duplicate PAIRS; run-to-run churn is 76% content-class (H119's in-prompt canonicalization cure REFUTED at -9.7%) and model-inherent (~0.60 JD, H229); exact-normalized name matches cover only 4.5% of real merge decisions (H267) - the resolver absorbs the froth by design (production resolution absorbed ~85% of R45 pass-2 emissions into existing identities)
- **Identity benchmark + state pinning (H101/H197)** - 298 adjudicated pairs with evidence strings are the resolver's ground truth; the 2 ms graph snapshot fingerprint is a standing precondition for any cross-day identity or recall comparison

- **Score-smoothed seed landing - the seed-RANKING mechanism (R54-H627; retrieval-level CONFIRMED 2026-07-23, end-to-end conversion pending H628)** - the first mechanism to move the dense carrier-recall bound that five encoders could not (H582: ~0.60 is EMBEDDER-INVARIANT). Carrier recall@16 0.6012 -> 0.6963 at alpha 0.5 (+9.51pts, paired bootstrap 95% CI [+6.13, +13.19], McNemar p ~ 0, 34 recovered against 3 diluted), peaking 0.7147 at alpha 0.6 (+11.35pts); the alpha is split-half stable - both halves of the frozen 132 independently select 0.6 and transfer held-out (0.7195 vs base 0.6173; 0.7099 vs base 0.5854). Mechanism: smooth the raw query-similarity vector one step over the normalized adjacency before taking the top-16, `s_pooled = [(1-alpha) s + alpha (Ahat s)] / ||z||`. This is ALGEBRAICALLY IDENTICAL (verified to 5.55e-16, top-16 sets agree exactly) to indexing a neighbour-pooled node vector `z_u = (1-alpha) e_u + alpha (Ahat e)_u`, because `<q, Ahat e>_u = (Ahat s)_u` - so the mechanism ships as a one-line score post-process with `||z||` precomputed as one scalar per node, NOT as a second vector index: no re-embedding, no storage doubling, no ingest coupling. WHY IT WORKS: a bridge entity's own text shares little with the question by definition, so the information needed to rank it is absent from the encoder's input - which is why a better encoder cannot help and H582's bound was invariant; smoothing lets a node inherit evidence from the neighbour that DOES match. Relation to the incumbent walk: PPR propagates a THRESHOLDED top-16 seed indicator to convergence, this propagates the FULL graded similarity signal exactly one step - same operator, different input and truncation, which is why the PPR control lands close (0.6564) and why smoothing dilutes 5x less (it never discards the sub-rank-16 signal). Bounds honestly stated: it is a RANKING lever inside the already-reachable region, NOT a reach lever - recoveries beyond seeds u 1-hop u PPR-top-15 are ZERO and cross-component recoveries are ZERO on every arm, because `Ahat^r` is block-diagonal on the 1,830-component archipelago exactly as R52-H598's heat kernel was; the +3.99pts over the PPR control has CI [-1.23, +8.90] straddling 0, so "beats PPR" is NOT established at n=326 - the defensible claim is a comparable gain at ingest-time cost instead of query-time cost. Two-hop smoothing is dead flat (+0.00pts at every alpha - `Ahat^2` washes out at this sparsity); the un-normalized 1-hop-restrict-and-rerank control is exactly 0.00, proving the gain is neighbour EVIDENCE and not region restriction. Ceiling banked: the single-neighbour inheritance oracle reads 0.8190, so the realizable mean cashes 44% of the mechanism and ~12pts of ranked headroom remains (H630).
